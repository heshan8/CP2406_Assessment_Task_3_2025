## Parameters used for testing

1. **NUM_BODIES** - Number of particles in the disk
2. **SYSTEM_SIZE_AU** - View radius (zoom level) in Astronomical Units
3. **DISK_MASS_FRAC** - Fraction of total mass in the disk (vs central star)
